🤔 What is Algorithmic Thinking?

It's the superpower of breaking down any task so clearly that even B.O.B. can't mess it up! Core concepts include:

  • 🧩 Decomposition: Breaking big tasks into smaller steps.
  • 🔢 Sequencing: Putting actions in the exact logical order.
  • 🎯 Precision: Being incredibly specific.
  • 🕵️ Assumption Checking: Never assuming B.O.B. knows preconditions.
  • 🔁 Repetition: Looping actions efficiently.
  • ⚖️ Decision Making: Using "if/then" to handle different situations.
  • 🔄 Iteration: Testing, failing, and improving!

📋 Execution Report

❌ What Went Wrong?

B.O.B. tried to spread peanut butter, but the jar was still closed.

🧠 Algorithmic Thinking Connection (Avoiding Assumptions)

You assumed the peanut butter jar was already open. Algorithmic thinkers try to identify assumptions and make important actions explicit.

🛠️ How an Algorithmic Thinker Approaches This

Before writing steps, they identify state. "Is the jar open or closed?" If closed, "Open jar" MUST be a step before "Insert knife."

💡 Better Example

"1. Twist the lid counter-clockwise to open the peanut butter jar. 2. Dip the knife..."
